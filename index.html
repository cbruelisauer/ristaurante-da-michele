Owner & Head Chef

Michael
"Michele" Hersche

Michael grew up in Zürich but learned to bake in Naples, where an apprenticeship under a third-generation pizzaiolo turned into an obsession with one question: what happens to flour and water if you simply give them enough time.

He brought that answer home. The nickname "Michele" stuck after his first summer working the ovens on the Amalfi coast, and it's the one that now hangs above the door on Schweighofstrasse. He still shapes the first dozen pizze of every service himself.

— Michele
